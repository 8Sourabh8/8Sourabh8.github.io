RTL phase controller for RIS-assisted mmWave MIMO

At 28 GHz one building can block the signal. A Reconfigurable Intelligent Surface steers it around the obstacle, but only if every element gets the right phase in time. Papers assume those phases are simply set. I designed the hardware that sets them.

  • Verilog datapath: codebook ROM, control FSM, serial shift register and output latch.
  • Verified in Vivado: 18 of 18 tests pass.
  • In MATLAB, reproduced 1-bit and 2-bit phase quantisation loss from an IEEE paper and raised coverage behind a building from 8.8% to 100%.
18 / 18tests passed
1.31 µsper beam update
157 LUT · 147 FFon Artix-7 at 100 MHz
~3×2×2 MIMO capacity gain

1x3 Router

RTL + UVM

A packet router that sends each incoming packet to one of three output FIFOs based on its header address.

  • Verilog RTL: FSM, synchroniser, register block and three FIFOs, linted with SpyGlass.
  • UVM environment with source and destination agents, virtual sequencer and scoreboard.
  • Tests for each destination and for FIFO soft reset; cross coverage on address, payload size and error.

AHB-to-APB Bridge

UVM

Verification of the bridge that converts pipelined AMBA AHB transfers into two-phase APB transfers for low-speed peripherals.

  • AHB master agent and APB slave agent with configurable environment.
  • Single and burst transfer sequences driven through a virtual sequencer.
  • Scoreboard checks address, slave select and byte-lane data for every transfer.

Research & talks

Before VLSI, I spent my undergraduate years in the Nano-Club at KLS Gogte Institute of Technology, working on sensors and sustainable farming.

Sep 2022 – Jan 2023 · Nano-Club, KLS GIT

Electrochemical detection of the herbicide molinate using ZnO nanoparticles

Molinate is widely used in rice fields. We modified a carbon paste electrode with zinc oxide nanoparticles and studied molinate with cyclic voltammetry, then tested the method on spiked soil and water samples.

10 nMdetection limit
91.7–99.5%recovery in real samples
Dec 2023 – Apr 2024 · UG major project

Frequency-based electro-culture for chemical-free farming

A copper antenna that draws ions from the atmosphere and channels them into the soil. We tested it on turnip plants across water content, chlorophyll (SPAD), height, biomass and leaf area, and saw better growth with no chemical fertilisers.
